A few requests

House rules

01
No smoking inside the unit

Cigarettes are not allowed indoors; vaping is allowed. A ₱1,000 charge applies for any smoking violation.

02
Quiet hours: 9PM–6AM

Please keep music and voices low so the neighbouring units can rest.

03
Swimming attire only at the pool

Strictly swimming attire in the pool area.

04
No food or alcohol at the pool

Please enjoy food and drinks inside the unit instead.

05
Pool hours: 6AM–10PM

Kids may swim until 6PM only.

06
Do not log out of Netflix

Please leave the smart TV signed in to our account.

07
Nothing attached to the walls

Do not attach, stick or hang posters, decorations, hooks or tape using adhesives, glue or nails. Wall damage carries a ₱1,000 charge.

08
Wash your dishes

Please clean up after cooking or eating. A ₱100 fine applies for dishes left unwashed.

09
No unregistered visitors

Only registered guests may enter the unit. A ₱1,000 fine applies.

10
No cooking fish or strong-smelling food

A ₱500 fine applies — the smell lingers for the next guests.

11
Bring your own personal items

Toiletries, slippers and personal hygiene kits are not provided.

12
Towel rental

₱50 per towel — just message us if you need one.

13
Proper swim attire only

Nylon, rash guard or lycra. No cotton or maong (denim) in the pool.
